## Design Context

### Users

Signal Foundry is for enterprise employees, team leads, AI enablement owners, risk reviewers, and Microsoft hackathon judges. Employees use it inside Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat to discover role-relevant AI capabilities; reviewers use it to approve, reject, or refine proposed capabilities; judges use the Foundry Floor command center to verify Copilot, Work IQ, MCP, OAuth, and audit-safe behavior.

### Brand Personality

Controlled, inventive, industrial, and trustworthy. The experience should feel like an enterprise foundry for approved AI workflows: confident enough for executives, practical enough for frontline teams, and distinctive enough to be remembered in a hackathon judging room.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the provided Signal Foundry brand reference: graphite, warm steel, electric teal, amber, a hexagonal forge/anvil mark, raw signal traces entering from the left, and approved workflow tiles exiting to the right. Avoid generic dashboards, glassmorphism, stock illustrations, decorative gradients, identical metric-card grids, and generic AI imagery. The visual metaphor is transformation: raw work signals are forged with intelligence into approved workflows.

### Design Principles

1. Copilot first: the agent interaction is the product surface; the frontend proves and visualizes what happened.
2. Capability over surveillance: show workflows, approvals, risks, and releases, not employee behavior tracking.
3. Registry as product: every valuable idea becomes a durable, auditable capability record.
4. One unforgettable artifact: the Signal Atlas / Foundry Floor must be the demo moment.
5. Audit-safe by default: no raw Microsoft 365 content, secrets, tokens, or private user data in UI, logs, screenshots, or generated artifacts.
